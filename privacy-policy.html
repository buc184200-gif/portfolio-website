Crestiva Web Studio Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Crestiva Web Studio ("Crestiva", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we may collect from you, how it is used, and how it is protected when you visit our website or communicate with us.

2. Information We May Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily submit to us when inquiring about our services or requesting project discussions. This information may include:

  • Your name and contact details (such as email address and phone number).
  • Business name, business country, website URL, and current online presence details.
  • Project specifications, design preferences, and estimated budget selections.
  • Country-level location information: Approximate country codes (e.g. IN, US, GB, AE, AU) provided through server/edge network context strictly to display regional pricing. We do not request or use browser GPS coordinates or physical street addresses.
  • Any other details or communications you provide through our contact forms, email, or direct messaging channels.

3. Approximate Location and Regional Pricing

To display region-appropriate pricing, Crestiva Web Studio may use approximate country-level information provided by our hosting infrastructure based on network request information such as an IP-derived country code.

We do not request or use precise GPS location for regional pricing. Visitors are never prompted for device location access permissions to view pricing on our website.

Crestiva uses this country-level information only to determine the pricing region shown on the website and does not intentionally store the visitor's raw IP address for this pricing purpose.

Our hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses, as necessary to deliver, secure, and operate the website infrastructure.

Regional pricing displayed on the website is indicative and may be confirmed against the client's actual business location during consultation.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely for genuine business purposes, including:

  • Responding promptly to your inquiries and consultation requests.
  • Discussing, estimating, and scoping website design and development projects.
  • Delivering, developing, and optimizing approved client deliverables.
  • Sending project-related communications, proposals, updates, and deliverables.
  • Improving our studio communication, client service delivery, and website experience.

5. Contact Forms and Communications

When you submit an inquiry through our website contact form or initiate contact via email or WhatsApp, your submitted information (name, email, phone, business name, business country, and project message) is used exclusively to respond to your request, coordinate project discussions, and manage client communication. We do not use your contact information for unsolicited marketing or sell it to mailing lists.

6. Payments

Crestiva Web Studio may require a 25% advance before work begins on applicable services and projects. Payments are arranged directly with the client and are not processed through a website payment gateway. There is no live payment gateway or customer checkout on this website, and we do not collect or store credit card numbers, banking credentials, or payment account secrets through the site.

7. Third-Party Services

We only engage trusted infrastructure and communication platforms necessary to deliver our website and manage client discussions:

  • Netlify: Provides cloud hosting, edge function routing, and approximate country code detection for regional pricing.
  • Supabase: Provides secure database storage for contact form inquiries submitted voluntarily by prospective clients.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): Provides direct messaging when visitors choose to initiate project discussions via our WhatsApp links.

We do not integrate invasive third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioral analytics.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party profiling scripts, or behavioral tracking mechanisms. Any standard technical cookies or server logging used are strictly for basic website delivery, reliability, and security.

9. Local Storage / Session Data

Our website may use standard browser local storage or session storage solely to temporarily preserve interactive UI states (such as custom quote calculator inputs) on your local device. We do not store manual pricing overrides or visitor tracking profiles in browser storage.

10. Data Sharing

Crestiva Web Studio does not sell, rent, trade, or lease your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared where reasonably necessary to provide agreed client services, operate essential communications, or fulfill legal obligations.

11. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational precautions to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely infallible, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. Data Retention

We retain submitted contact and project information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill inquiry responses, deliver ongoing client projects, maintain administrative records, and satisfy legal or contractual requirements.

13. User Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided to us. To submit a request or inquire about your data, please contact us directly using the contact details below.

14. Children's Privacy

Our website and professional services are intended for businesses and adults, and are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Crestiva Web Studio reserves the right to update or revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of our website or services signifies your acceptance of any updates.

16. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at: crestivawebstudio@gmail.com
